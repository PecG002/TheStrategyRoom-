FAQ

What people ask in the room.

Clear answers to practical questions about business systems and how we work.

What exactly does The Strategy Room do?

We help businesses examine how they currently operate and build clearer structures, processes, workflows and oversight systems around the work.

What is a business system?

A business system is a deliberate way of organising how a part of the business works—who owns it, what happens, what information moves and how the work is monitored.

Does my business need systems if I'm still small?

Small businesses can benefit from clarity early. The point is not to overcomplicate the business; it is to avoid building a company that can only function through one person's memory.

Do you only work with large companies?

No. We work with businesses and founders at different stages, particularly where growth or complexity is exposing operational gaps.

Do you implement systems or only provide recommendations?

The Strategy Room is positioned around building practical systems, not simply describing what a business should do. The specific scope depends on the engagement.

How do I know what systems my business needs?

You do not need to diagnose the whole business before contacting us. The conversation can begin with what currently feels difficult, unclear or overly dependent on you.

How long does building a system take?

That depends on the business, the scope and the complexity of the system. We avoid giving one universal timeline for work that may differ significantly between organisations.

Can you help if my business is already experiencing operational chaos?

Yes. Chaos is often useful information: it can reveal where roles, processes, information flow or oversight are not keeping up with the business.
