About The Strategy Room

Businesses should not have to live inside the founder's head.

The Strategy Room is a business systems and strategy partner focused on helping businesses create the structure required to operate with greater clarity, accountability and control.

Who we are

A room for the work behind the business.

Many businesses begin with the founder doing almost everything. Decisions happen quickly. Processes are remembered. People simply ask whoever knows.

But growth changes the operating reality. More people, customers and responsibilities create dependencies. What once worked through memory and constant founder involvement eventually becomes difficult to manage.

The Strategy Room exists for the work of making that transition deliberate.

PEOPLE+PROCESSES+OVERSIGHT=A WAY OF WORKING
The person behind the room

Norah Akinyi Otieno

The Strategy Room was established around a simple observation: ambition alone cannot hold a growing business together.

As a business becomes more complex, the founder needs more than effort. The business needs clarity about who owns what, how work moves, where information goes and how leadership knows whether things are working.

That philosophy sits at the centre of The Strategy Room: move important knowledge out of one person's head and into structures people can actually use.

Mission

To help businesses build practical systems that create clarity, accountability and sustainable growth.

Vision

A business landscape where businesses are built to function, grow and thrive beyond the founder.

Who we serve

Businesses at the point where informal ways of working are no longer enough.

Founder-led businesses

For founders who have become the decision-maker, memory bank and problem-solver for everything.

Growing teams

For businesses adding people faster than they are adding clarity.

Service businesses

For teams that need consistent delivery, clearer handovers and dependable workflows.

Small businesses

For businesses that want to build structure before complexity becomes expensive.

Businesses preparing to scale

For organisations that know growth will expose the weaknesses of the current setup.

Operationally stretched teams

For businesses experiencing recurring confusion, bottlenecks and founder dependency.

The point

From “everything is in my head” to “everything has a system.”
