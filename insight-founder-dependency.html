Why Your Business Should Not Depend on Your Memory

Founders often become the unofficial operating system of the business. They remember the client details, the process, the exception and the next step.

This can work when the business is small. It becomes increasingly fragile as the number of people and responsibilities grows.

Memory is not a scalable operating model.

The goal is not to remove the founder from the business. The goal is to stop requiring the founder to personally hold every critical detail together.
