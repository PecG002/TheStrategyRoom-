The Hidden Cost of Unclear Roles

When something goes wrong in a business with unclear roles, the first question is often: “Who was supposed to do this?”

That question usually arrives too late.

Clear roles make ownership visible before the problem appears. They help people understand where a responsibility starts, where it ends and when another person should take over.

Without that clarity, businesses pay through duplicated work, delayed decisions and accountability that becomes difficult to locate.
