Growth Doesn't Fix Chaos. It Magnifies It.

Growth is often treated as the solution to a business problem. More customers. More employees. More revenue.

But growth also increases the number of handovers, decisions and opportunities for a weak process to break.

A business that is unclear at one level can become significantly more chaotic at a larger level.

The work of building systems is therefore not separate from growth. It is part of preparing the business to absorb growth.
